# Stadig — Design Rules

## Aesthetic & Vibe
- **Minimal, clean, and warm** — Scandinavian sensibility throughout
- Feels human and approachable, not corporate or sterile
- A hint of character — small details that feel alive, but never distracting
- The studio has a quiet confidence: the work speaks, the brand supports

---

## Color
- **Light theme only** — never pure white or pure black
- Use **cream/off-white** as the background base
- Use **deep, warm brown/dark** as the primary text color for eye comfort and contrast
- Accent colors **vary by context**: different for Stadig's own pages vs. client project pages
- No gradients

---

## Typography
- **Fonts**: Manrope + Inter — both geometric sans-serif, keep these
- **Headlines**: Large but not overwhelming — commanding yet restrained
- **Hierarchy**: Clear and balanced, not cinematic or poster-style
- **Tone of copy**: Warm and conversational — feels like a person talking, approachable, no fluff

---

## Layout & Spacing
- **Lots of whitespace** — generous breathing room, nothing crowded
- **Rhythm-based spacing** — spacing follows a consistent vertical rhythm, nothing ad hoc
- **Clear column structure** — you can feel the grid even when it's not visible
- Images follow the margin by default — unless explicitly specified otherwise

---

## Grid & Structure
- Clear columns with a felt but not rigid structure
- Asymmetry is allowed but structure must be intentional
- No decorative elements — no lines, dividers, ornaments, or shapes
- Let layout and typography do all the work

---

## Images
- Default: contained within margins, respecting the grid
- Exceptions allowed case by case when explicitly specified
- No stock photo aesthetic — everything should feel authentic and considered

---

## UI Elements

### Buttons
- Default: **rounded / pill-shaped**
- No borders — solid fill or no border at all depending on context
- Rule applies across all pages unless explicitly overridden

### Cards & Containers
- **No drop shadows**
- Slightly rounded corners on images — very subtle, barely noticeable
- Buttons fully rounded

### Navigation
- **Minimal top bar** — just a few links, very unobtrusive
- Sticky navigation preferred

### Footer
- Minimal — just essentials (copyright, few links)
- No elaborate content or large CTAs

---

## Motion & Interaction
- **Smooth and purposeful** — elegant transitions that guide attention
- **Hover state**: Scale / zoom — elements gently grow on hover
- No flashy or playful animation
- Nothing should feel arbitrary — every motion has a reason

---

## Mobile
- **Both mobile and desktop are equally important** — full experience on both without compromise

---

## Case Studies / Project Pages
- **Quick overview format** — short, fast to scan, no deep dives
- Image-forward, but not at the expense of context

---

## What to Avoid
- Cluttered layouts — no visual noise or overwhelming density
- Stock photo aesthetic — nothing generic or corporate-looking
- Drop shadows
- Gradients
- Pure white or pure black — always use warm near-neutrals
- Decorative elements (lines, shapes, ornaments)

---

## References & Inspiration
- Scandinavian design studios (e.g. Snask, Kurppa Hosk, Hej Studio)
- Scandinavian design principles: restraint, warmth, function, quiet confidence
